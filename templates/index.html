Core Architecture

Designed for Fast, Accountable Resolutions

Everything needed to transform chaotic campus maintenance complaints into an organized, transparent operational pipeline.

Smart Keyword Classification

Rule-based natural language parser analyzes complaint title and text to categorize issues into Electricity, Water, Wi-Fi, Infrastructure, etc. without paid APIs.

Automated Priority Triage

Urgent safety risks like electrical sparks, smoke, gas leaks, or stuck elevators are immediately flagged as Urgent for emergency dispatch.

Unique ID Complaint Tracking

Every report receives a verifiable tracking ID like CC-2026-1001. Students and staff can track real-time lifecycle progression at any time.

Visual Evidence Uploads

Attach photo evidence of damaged infrastructure, broken pipes, or faulty hardware with preview capabilities for technician inspection.

Visual Analytics Dashboard

Interactive Chart.js visualizations for administrators to monitor category distributions, resolution rates, and department maintenance trends.

Live Campus Announcements

Broadcast maintenance schedules, power outage alerts, and campus-wide updates directly to student dashboards with priority tags.

Workflow Lifecycle

How CampusCare Works

From initial problem discovery to final technician resolution in 4 simple steps.

01

Report & Snap

Student submits the issue with title, description, building location, and optional photo attachment.

02

Smart Triage

Keyword heuristic engine calculates category confidence and auto-assigns urgent or high priority based on safety criteria.

03

Action & Dispatch

Administrators review reports on the centralized portal, update status to 'In Progress', and record technician notes.

04

Resolved & Verified

Once fixed, status changes to 'Resolved', recording exact completion timestamps for students to verify on their tracking timeline.
