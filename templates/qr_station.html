How to Test with Your Smartphone Camera

When running on localhost (`127.0.0.1`), clicking the "Test Link" buttons immediately launches the report form with the exact location pre-filled. To test with your actual physical smartphone camera:

  1. Ensure your computer and phone are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Run Flask with your machine's local IP (e.g., `python app.py` running on `192.168.x.x:5000`).
  3. Point your phone camera at any QR code on this screen. Your phone will immediately pop up a banner to lodge the grievance!
